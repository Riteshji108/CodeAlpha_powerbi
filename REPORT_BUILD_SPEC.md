# Power BI Report Build Specification

## TASK 1 — Financial Health Dashboard
Pages:
1. Executive: Revenue, Gross Margin %, EBIT Margin %, Net Margin %, Ending Cash, Free Cash Flow.
2. Statements: monthly Revenue/COGS/Opex/EBIT/Net Income plus cash flow visual.
3. Balance Sheet: Cash, AR, Inventory, PPE, AP and Debt.
4. Forecast: Analytics-pane forecast on Revenue and Net Cash Flow for budgeting/planning.

## TASK 2 — Human Resources Analytics
Pages:
1. Workforce: active headcount, turnover rate, satisfaction and performance by department.
2. Recruitment: Applications → Interviews → Offers → Hires funnel, trend, and department view.
3. Retention: satisfaction/performance against turnover and exits.
4. Hiring Needs: open requisition trend plus moving-average hiring target.

## TASK 3 — Real Estate Market Trends
Pages:
1. Market Overview: average/median price, rental yield, demand, supply, price/sqft.
2. City Hotspots: map using Latitude/Longitude, bubble size = property price, color = demand-supply gap.
3. Investment: rental yield vs price and demand vs supply scatter by property type.
4. Economics: GDP growth and inflation comparisons by city.

Use slicers for dates/city/department/property type as relevant.
